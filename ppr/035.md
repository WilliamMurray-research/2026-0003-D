1. System Overview & Spatial Hierarchy
The Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning (HRL) platform optimizes spatial water management and climate adaptation interventions across complex deltaic systems. The architecture operates across a two-tier spatial and temporal hierarchy to decouple macro-level hydrological allocation from micro-level physical execution.

6.2 Phase D Exit Gates for Full Scale Deployment
To receive authorization for full spatial rollout (Phase E), the pilot deployment must clear four quantitative gates over the 24-month testing window:
 * HydroECA Gain: Realized network connectivity in treatment units must exceed synthetic controls by \ge 18\% (p < 0.01).
 * Model Accuracy: Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) between predicted and observed HydroECA must remain < 12\%.
 * Governance Pipeline Health: Zero treatment cells remain trapped in exploration deadlock without physical deployment beyond 9\text{ months}.
 * Salinity Mitigation: Agricultural soil salinity in designated protection zones must show a statistically significant reduction of \ge 2.5\text{ dS/m} during dry season peak.
